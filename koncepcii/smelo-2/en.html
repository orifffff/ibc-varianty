Services

  1. 1Market analytics and researchMarket and feasibility studies, industry reports and land potential assessments
    • Strategic and market research on industrial and warehouse real estate at the client’s request
    • Preparation of industry reports, expert publications and analytical materials
    • Expert assessment of a land plot’s development potential and efficiency of use
    • Project feasibility studies
    • Participation in industry events and initiatives
  2. 2Brokerage servicesLeasing, sales and acquisitions: tenant and buyer search, land and developer sourcing, lease negotiations
    • Leasing, sales and acquisitions of real estate assets
    • Exclusive asset marketing and project pre-brokerage
    • Sourcing tenants and buyers for real estate assets
    • Sourcing real estate assets for purchase or lease
    • Sourcing land plots and developers to meet specified requirements; turnkey construction of facilities
    • Supporting parties in negotiations, optimizing lease agreements
    • Developing positioning and pricing strategies
  3. 3Strategic and financial advisoryBusiness strategies, financial models, financing and deal structuring
    • Developing business strategies and financial models for market participants and investors
    • Raising financing and structuring transactions
    • Support for large-scale development and investment projects
    • Full advisory support at any stage of the project
  4. 4Land development and designSite assessment, permits, concept and detailed design, value engineering and warehouse flow design
    • Assessing the site and market conditions; creating optimal development conditions
    • Managing the land development process, preparing project plans and obtaining permits
    • Developing concept designs, preliminary and detailed designs
    • Reviewing existing preliminary/detailed designs for optimization (value engineering)
    • Designing warehouse flow concepts, processes and technology integration
    • Sourcing investors and budgeting
  5. 5Construction project managementBuild-to-suit delivery with control of schedule, budget and quality
    • End-to-end support for construction projects
    • Project delivery to the client’s requirements (build-to-suit)
    • Project delivery management: control of schedule, budget and quality
  6. 6Facility & property management (new service line)Leasing, rent collection, building operations, maintenance and cleaning
    • Integrated management of the building’s operations, systems and infrastructure
    • Property management: leasing of premises, rent collection, tenant relations
    • Facility management: receiving, storage, order picking, dispatch, security and regulatory compliance
    • Maintenance and cleaning services
    • A suite of services for the most efficient use of the property in the owner’s interest
  7. 7Marketing and media relationsBranding and naming, digital marketing, PR campaigns and events
    • Market research and strategic planning of marketing communications
    • Turnkey brand creation: branding and naming of the asset
    • Digital marketing and promotion of real estate assets
    • Media relations support, organizing and running PR campaigns
    • Organizing and running events tailored to the client’s objectives
